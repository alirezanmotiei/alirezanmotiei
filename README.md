### 🧬 About & Research Statement

I am an Electrical Engineering graduate and researcher from the **University of Tehran**, ranked **10th out of 120** in my cohort with a **3.89 / 4.00 (18.32 / 20)** GPA. 

My research lies at the intersection of **hardware bioelectronics** and **computational intelligence**:
1. **Bio-Signal Instrumentation & Co-Culture Platforms**: Designing high-frequency analog/digital circuitry (100 kHz - 500 kHz) and PDMS/PMMA co-culture chambers with patterned Au/Cr interdigitated electrodes (IDEs) to deliver Tumor Treating Fields (TTFields).
2. **Deep Generative Modeling & Representation Learning**: Developing and benchmarking score-based diffusion models (VE-SDEs in the complex STFT domain), Normalizing Flows, and Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) for biomedical signal separation and scientific representation learning.
